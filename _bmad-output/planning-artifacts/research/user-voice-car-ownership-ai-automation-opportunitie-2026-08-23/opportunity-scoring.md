## Scoring Rationale

### AI Repair Advocate

- Extends existing service history and schedules at the exact moment an owner must authorize expensive work.
- AI can extract and explain estimate line items, compare them with stored history and due tasks, identify duplicates or missing detail, and generate questions.
- Savings are plausible through avoided duplicate, premature, unauthorized, or poorly explained work, but no fixed savings should be promised.
- Trust is the central risk: the feature must not diagnose, declare work unnecessary, or present unaudited market prices as facts.

### Ownership Admin Concierge

- Tracks warranties, service contracts, roadside coverage, insurance renewal, recalls, registration, and cancellation/refund deadlines.
- Strongly reflects the affluent “one point of contact” advantage, but a broad first release risks becoming document storage with reminders.

### Recall Resolution Concierge

- Strong public data and clear safety value, especially for used vehicles.
- Free government and commercial tools already provide lookup/alerts; differentiation requires follow-through workflows, not detection alone.

### Preventable Breakdown Coach

- High-volume pain and excellent fit with existing maintenance data.
- Without telematics, inspection results, or OBD hardware, predictions would mostly repackage schedules and risk generic advice.

### AI Resale Concierge

- Can turn maintenance records into a buyer-ready dossier, draft listings, suggest photo checklists, and organize inquiries.
- Valuable but episodic, with weaker daily-life relevance than repair authorization.

### Insurance Renewal Copilot

- Strong comprehension and potential savings problem.
- Quote access, province/state rules, licensed activity, and rapidly changing policy terms make a trustworthy MVP harder. Calendar/document explanation alone may not outperform existing brokers.

### Car Buying and F&I Guard

- Large transaction-level upside and clear evidence of overwhelm.
- Episodic, data-hungry, and already served by focused AI/human products; weak fit with the current post-purchase Car module.

## Provisional MVP Boundary

Upload or paste a repair estimate and receive:

1. Structured line-item extraction.
2. Plain-language explanations and a confidence indicator.
3. Cross-checks against the vehicle's logged history, current due tasks, and duplicate services.
4. Flags for missing detail, bundled charges, conflicting quantities, and work added without a recorded reason.
5. A prioritized “ask the shop” checklist and second-opinion handoff summary.
6. Explicit boundaries: not a diagnosis, not legal advice, and no claim that a repair is unnecessary solely from paperwork.

Live regional price benchmarking, shop booking, negotiation, insurance claims, and mechanical diagnosis stay outside the first MVP.
