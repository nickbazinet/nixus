# Research Brief: Car Ownership AI Automation Opportunities

## Decision

Which feature should Nixus add next to its Car module to improve ordinary car owners' day-to-day lives or save them money by democratizing advantages commonly available to affluent owners?

## Current Product Boundary

Nixus is a local-first desktop app. The Car module already provides a multi-vehicle garage, maintenance schedules, service history, odometer tracking, and due-date alerts. Recommendations must extend this foundation rather than duplicate it.

## Research Dimensions

1. **Owner pain and workarounds:** costly, recurring, or stressful jobs car owners struggle to perform well.
2. **Affluent-owner advantages:** concierge, expert, administrative, negotiation, preventative, and information services that money currently buys.
3. **Existing solutions and gaps:** current consumer products, service models, pricing, complaints, and unserved needs.
4. **Feature selection:** opportunities where AI or automation can create trustworthy value in a local-first desktop product.

## Decision Criteria

Score each credible opportunity from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong):

- User pain frequency and severity
- Plausible direct savings or loss avoidance
- Automation/AI leverage over a static tracker
- Fit with Nixus's existing data and local-first model
- User trust and explainability
- MVP feasibility for a solo developer (higher is easier)
- Differentiation from free reminders and existing apps

Savings claims must distinguish demonstrated savings from plausible loss avoidance. Recommendations must disclose dependencies on live pricing, telematics, mobile capture, external APIs, or jurisdiction-specific data.

## Evidence Plan

- Use current primary sources where possible: regulators, public agencies, consumer organizations, and vendors' own product/pricing pages.
- Triangulate owner sentiment across independent communities or review surfaces before making prevalence claims.
- Treat vendor marketing as capability evidence, not proof of outcomes.
- Independently verify every recommendation-driving claim under normal validation.

## Deliverable

A concise cited report containing owner jobs-to-be-done, affluent advantages worth democratizing, a ranked feature matrix, one recommended MVP, a validation experiment, contrary evidence, open questions, and a source freshness map.
