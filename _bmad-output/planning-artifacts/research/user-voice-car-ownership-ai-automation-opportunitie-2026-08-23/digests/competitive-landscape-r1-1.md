## Leads and Gaps

- Competitors specialize by lane: diagnostics, repair pricing, insurance, or buying. No cleanly evidenced product combines persistent ownership records with transparent decision support across repair estimates and ownership administration.
- Canada appears underserved, but product absence requires targeted verification before becoming a market claim.
- Trust failures cluster around billing, quote transparency, and marketplace promises.
- Openbay status remains disputed; do not use it as a recommendation-driving fact.
