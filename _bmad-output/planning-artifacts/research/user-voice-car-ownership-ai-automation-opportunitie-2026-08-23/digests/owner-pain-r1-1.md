# Car Owner Pain Digest

Accessed: 2026-08-23. Claims below were retrieved this run.

1. **Repair spending and trust vary materially by service channel.** Average reported transaction values were US$521.69 at franchised dealers, $334.33 at independents, $270.52 at chains, and $106.44 at quick-lube shops; first-visit fix rates were 63%, 62%, and 48% for dealers, independents, and chains. Source: https://www.duckercarlisle.com/about/news/chains-overtake-dealers-as-top-service-provider/; publisher: Ducker Carlisle; published 2026-06-16; confidence: high; class: market survey; geography: US.
2. **Owners report unnecessary repairs, overcharging, unauthorized work, and quote-to-invoice changes, but the available prevalence survey is stale.** Source: https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/car-repair/; publisher: AARP citing a 1,000-owner survey; published 2023-11-20; confidence: medium; class: survey. Current category corroboration: https://www.mechanicinsights.com/2026/05/car-repair-estimate-red-flags.html; published 2026-05-01; confidence: medium.
3. **Recall completion is materially worse for older vehicles and subsequent owners.** NHTSA reports a 65.8% weighted completion rate for 2012-2022 vehicle-only recalls, falling from 83% for newer vehicles to 44% for vehicles aged 5-10 years; only 18% of surveyed owners had used NHTSA's lookup, and roughly 40% of non-completers cited inconvenience. Source: https://www.nhtsa.gov/document/report-congress-improving-vehicle-safety-recall-completion-rates; publisher: NHTSA; published 2026-04-01; confidence: high; class: regulatory data; geography: US.
4. **Policy comprehension, not just price, is a major insurance pain.** Only 58% of surveyed customers said they completely understood their auto policy; full understanding correlated with 127-point higher overall satisfaction and 141-point higher price satisfaction. Roughly one-third of shoppers used AI comparison tools. Source: https://www.jdpower.com/business/press-releases/2026-us-auto-insurance-study; publisher: J.D. Power; published 2026-06-09; confidence: high; class: market survey; geography: US.
5. **Insurers may withhold individual renewal-pricing detail as commercially sensitive.** Source examples: https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/decision/DRN-4574658.pdf and https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/decision/DRN-5093994.pdf; publisher: UK Financial Ombudsman Service; 2024-2026; confidence: high but jurisdiction-limited; class: adjudicated cases.
6. **Extended-warranty and maintenance-plan upselling increased while trust declined and buyer overwhelm rose.** Source: https://www.cdkglobal.com/insights/new-report-shows-shifts-fi-trust; publisher: CDK Global; published 2025-09-17; confidence: high; class: market survey; geography: US. Qualitative owner discussions independently illustrate regret and claims friction, but are not used for prevalence.
7. **Dealership add-on pressure and obstructed cancellation/refunds are documented consumer harms.** Sources: https://news.dealershipguy.com/p/car-buyers-want-two-simple-things-from-dealers-clear-prices-and-no-games-survey-2025-12-19 and https://www.consumerfinancialserviceslawmonitor.com/2024/10/cfpbs-fall-edition-of-supervisory-highlights-focuses-on-auto-finance-market/; publishers: Dealership Guy/YouGov summary and CFPB-summary legal publication; published 2025-12-19 and 2024-10-08; confidence: medium for survey, high for regulatory findings; class: survey/regulatory.
8. **Breakdowns are high-volume and concentrated in partly preventable categories.** AAA handled more than 27 million roadside calls in 2024; towing (~13M) and battery issues (~7M) comprised about 74%, and AAA explicitly connected many calls to deferred maintenance. Source: https://newsroom.aaa.com/2025/04/aaa-urges-drivers-to-stay-proactive-on-auto-repair-and-maintenance/; publisher: AAA; published 2025-04-01; confidence: high; class: operator statistics; geography: US/Canada footprint.
9. **Digital-tool unfamiliarity correlates with car-buying stress.** In a 2,042-buyer survey, unfamiliar buyers more often reported overwhelm, stress, confusion, intimidation, and frustration. Source: https://www.capitalone.com/about/newsroom/2025-car-buying-outlook-report/; publisher: Capital One; published 2025-12-04; confidence: medium-high; class: sponsored market survey; geography: US.

## Leads and Gaps

- Strongest problem signals: repair-estimate opacity, recall follow-through, insurance comprehension, F&I/add-on complexity, and preventable breakdowns.
- No fresh rigorous North American survey directly quantifies repair overcharging prevalence.
- Canada-specific quantified evidence is thin.
- Records/documentation at resale and fuel-cost tracking were not investigated in this round.
