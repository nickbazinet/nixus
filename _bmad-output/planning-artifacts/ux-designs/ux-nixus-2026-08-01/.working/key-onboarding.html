Key screen — Onboarding: one fork, then done

Replaces the shipped 5-step wizard (Budget → Accounts → Assets → Income → Import), whose step 1 asks for a budget from absolute zero at roughly 60–80 clicks. This is the fix for the north-star question, "what almost made you close it in the first 10 minutes?"

Governed by EXPERIENCE.md § Onboarding & Seeding, § Voice and Tone, § Key Flows / Flow 1. Final verified Direction A tokens.

Step 1 — Welcome

Nixus — 1024 × 680 (minimum window)

Let's get your money in one place

This takes about two minutes. Before you start, here's what Nixus does and doesn't do.

  • ·Your data stays on this computer. There's no account and nothing to sign up for.
  • ·Nixus doesn't connect to your bank. You'll upload statements yourself — a photo or a PDF is fine.
  • ·Nixus stays in your menu bar after you close the window, so reminders keep working. Quit it from there any time.
  • ·It's early days. Things will change and some things will break. Telling me what confused you is the most useful thing you can do.
Get started Read this later

Nixus 0.9 · pre-alpha

Why the limitations come first: the market research names "acknowledge limitations first" as a trust rule, and the tray behaviour is a required onboarding message per the alerts architecture — the app not quitting on window close is otherwise alarming.
