Design notes. Every formula is prose — "$14,320 in chequing and savings, against $4,455 of spending in a typical month" instead of liquid ÷ average monthly expenses. The trailing-average caveat is stated in words a person would use, not as "based on completed months." Only one rung is highlighted, and it's the only place the brand-tinted action-card treatment appears on the surface — which is what makes it read as do this. "Where you'd find the money" turns the abstract savings target into three concrete numbers, and the sub-nav label is "Where to put your money" rather than "Financial Health," because Marie isn't looking for a health score.
