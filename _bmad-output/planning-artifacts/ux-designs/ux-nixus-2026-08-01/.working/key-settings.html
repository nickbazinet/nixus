What gets sent

Only the statement you upload

When you import, the image or PDF goes to the service you chose above, and the transactions come back. Your budget, balances, accounts, and net worth are never sent.

Remember merchants I've categorized

Kept on this computer only. Next month, Nixus already knows that METRO is Groceries — so there's less to review each time.

"What gets sent" is the most important card on this screen and it doesn't exist in any prior spec. Nixus's entire positioning is local-first; a user who supplies an API key deserves an unambiguous statement of exactly what leaves the machine. It also quietly earns the merchant-memory toggle, which is otherwise an opaque setting.

Templates get a real home here, and the export path forced a decision worth logging: "Save your budget as a template" strips all amounts and keeps only category names. If community sharing is the eventual goal, a template that carried someone's mortgage payment would be a privacy incident waiting to happen. The format has to be amount-free by construction, not by user diligence. Also note restore leads with its warning above the control rather than in a confirm dialog after the fact.

What this mock illustrates: § IA → Settings as a rail destination with four sub-surfaces · § Component Patterns → Switch (missing primitive), segmented control, destructive action treatment · § Voice and Tone → consequences not preferences, plain language, no jargon · § Data Honesty Contract → explicit statement of what leaves the machine, no app-level encryption claim · § Onboarding & Seeding → template import/export as a portable file.

Decisions this mock forces into the log: (1) template export must be amount-free by construction; (2) the tray setting is user-controllable, not fixed; (3) merchant memory is a visible, explainable toggle; (4) "What gets sent" is a required card, not optional reassurance.
