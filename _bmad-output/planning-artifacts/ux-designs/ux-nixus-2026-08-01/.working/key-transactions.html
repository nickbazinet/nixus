Every element here is load-bearing. The header checkbox is indeterminate, not checked, because four of nine are selected — a detail that requires a real Checkbox primitive, which doesn't exist yet. Sortable headers carry ⇅ at rest and a direction arrow when active, and need aria-sort. The bulk bar states the selected sum as well as the count, because "4 selected" alone doesn't tell you whether you're about to delete $12 or $1,200. The account column shows the honest dash-plus-reason for the two rows that structurally cannot be linked. Amounts are inline-editable with a dotted underline. The footer restates the active filters in prose so a user who forgot what they filtered by isn't left guessing. No running-balance column — the data can't support one.
